# Smoky Motors vision

Smoky Motors makes a profit buying used cars (at auction, on Facebook, and through word of mouth) and reselling them. We compete on **fair prices and honesty**.

## What the website is for

**Now:** show the inventory on the lot so local buyers call, text, fill out the contact form, or come by.
**Later:** reach buyers farther away through SEO and AI-search optimization (AIO).

## What we sell

- Trucks.
- Affordable, reliable, low-mileage cars, such as the Ford Focus and Honda Civic.

## How the business runs

Smoky Motors is run mostly by AI with minimal human oversight. Humans do what has to be done in person:

- buying at auction
- test drives
- repairs, diagnosis, and inspections

Tony and Joe are the human face of Smoky Motors for customers. The site should make it easy to reach them.

## Ideas we welcome

- Anything that helps local buyers find a car, trust us, and get in touch: accurate listings, good photos, search and filters, clear contact paths on mobile, local SEO and AIO, accessibility, and speed.
- Anything that lets associates and AI keep the inventory accurate with less effort.
- Later: a basic chat agent on the website that answers shopper questions and hands off to Tony or Joe.

## We won't (for starters)

- Take payments or deposits online.
- Create shopper accounts or logins.
- Run credit applications.

**Never:** show the purchase price publicly. The purchase price is admin-only.

## Deferred

- Financing page.
- Suggested pricing from sale data.
- Scan-tool integration.

Deferred ideas are welcome as issues, but they wait for Ryan.

## How this file is used

This file is the judge for "should we build this?" for people and for MotorMane alike. If you think an idea should change this vision, ask Ryan. A declined idea means "not now," not "never."
